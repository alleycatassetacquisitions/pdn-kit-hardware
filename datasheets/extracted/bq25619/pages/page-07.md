###### **6.1 Absolute Maximum Ratings** 

over operating free-air temperature range (unless otherwise noted)<sup>(1)</sup> 


(1) Stresses beyond those listed under _Absolute Maximum Rating_ may cause permanent damage to the device. These are stress ratings only, which do not imply functional operation of the device at these or any other conditions beyond those indicated under _Recommended Operating Condition_ . Exposure to absolute-maximum-rated conditions for extended periods may affect device reliability. 

###### **6.2 ESD Ratings** 


(1) JEDEC document JEP155 states that 500-V HBM allows safe manufacturing with a standard ESD control process. 

(2) JEDEC document JEP157 states that 250-V CDM allows safe manufacturing with a standard ESD control process.
